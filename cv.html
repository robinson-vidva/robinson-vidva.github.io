Biomedical data scientist with more than 16 years of experience in multi-omics analysis, machine learning, and data analytics pipelines for computational oncology, immuno-oncology, drug discovery, and pediatric neurodevelopment. Integrates mutational, gene-expression, imaging, and clinical data to identify biomarkers of treatment response and to link molecular pathways to clinical and behavioral outcomes. Founded and led a computational biology consultancy, managed teams of more than 10 scientists, and established standards for model reproducibility and validation. Uses large language models (LLMs) and agentic coding tools in daily analysis and software development. Record: 7 journal articles, 2 preprints, 14 conference abstracts, 3 granted patents and 3 published applications, 150+ citations, 5 open-source tools including a laboratory platform in use at three laboratories.
