Focus areas — multi-omics, neurodevelopment, immuno-oncology, drug discovery, data science, and systems biology.

Computational biologist with 18+ years of experience in bioinformatics, multi-omics data analysis, neuroscience, immunology, immuno-oncology, and drug discovery. Research focuses on integrating transcriptomics, metabolomics, and genomics data to understand disease mechanisms and evaluate therapeutic interventions, with direct applications to improving pediatric health outcomes.

Across nearly two decades in academia and industry, he has led research teams and built the computational models and platforms that turn complex multi-omics data into biological insight and therapeutic direction — work reflected in peer-reviewed publications, granted patents in drug repurposing, and open-source tools for the wider research community.

Research Highlights

Neurodevelopmental data analysis

Applied multi-omics approaches to study preterm birth effects on brain development, and quantified behavioral deficits using DeepLabCut and B-SOiD in mouse models.

Immuno-oncology & precision medicine

Analyzed cancer genomics data across 500+ cell lines and immune-pathway interactions to identify therapeutic targets, contributing to precision-medicine advances.

Predictive disease modeling

Built pathway models for autoimmune diseases and cancer therapeutics, validated against patient datasets, and co-invented two granted patents in drug repurposing.

Drug discovery & combination design

Designed novel therapeutic combinations through computational analysis, and first-authored MyVivarium, an open-source cloud app for research animal colony management.
