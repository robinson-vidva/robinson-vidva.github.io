pathsim — signaling pathway simulator

Interactive simulations of canonical cell-signaling models. Move a parameter or a drug dose and the kinetics recompute live. Everything runs in your browser — no data leaves the page.

Model equations & method

Integrated with a fixed-step 4th-order Runge–Kutta scheme. Concentrations are conserved by construction; species are held non-negative.

These are canonical, illustrative models from the systems-biology literature, using published parameter sets. They demonstrate qualitative behaviour (ultrasensitivity, feedback oscillations, emergent drug response) — they are not calibrated to any specific cell line or patient and are for educational use only.
